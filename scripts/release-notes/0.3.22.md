## English

- Changed the activity Dock into a clear-glass floating capsule that can move freely in both axes and remembers its display and position. The Dock keeps app icons only.

- Simplified the web app top bar: close, pin, and bookmark remain on the left, with one vertical three-dot Kebab Menu on the right.
- Secondary actions now appear in a native menu with icons and labels. Bookmarks uses a ribbon-shaped bookmark icon.
- Download notices remain available in More Actions, with a small indicator on the button.

## 简体中文

- 活动 Dock 改为通透玻璃悬浮胶囊，可横向、纵向自由拖动并记住屏幕和位置；栏内只保留应用图标。

- 精简网页应用顶栏：左侧保留关闭、置顶和收藏，右侧只保留竖向三点的 Kebab Menu。
- 其他操作收进带图标和文字的系统菜单，收藏夹改用飘带书签图标。
- 下载提示保留在更多操作菜单内，并在按钮上显示小圆点提示。
