NeatWebApp macOS 0.3.30

- The activity Dock now appears where each web app window collapses: its top-leading corner lands on the collapsed window's top-leading corner (where the × button was), kept inside the screen. Dragging still works until the next collapse.
- The Dock's clear Liquid Glass stays visible over solid-color backgrounds: the native glass shadow is no longer clipped, and a thin rim that adapts to light and dark appearance outlines the capsule.

---

- 活动 Dock 现在会出现在每次网页应用窗口收起的位置：Dock 左上角对准收起窗口的左上角（原 × 按钮处），并保持在屏幕内。仍可手动拖动，直到下一次收起。
- 纯色背景上也能看清 Dock 的通透液态玻璃：系统自带的玻璃阴影不再被裁掉，并加了一圈随浅色/深色外观变化的细边勾出轮廓。
